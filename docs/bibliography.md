# Bibliography

References for every method and metric implemented in reguq. If you use a method, cite its paper.

## Conformal prediction

- Vovk, V., Gammerman, A. & Shafer, G. (2005). *Algorithmic Learning in a Random World*. Springer.
- Papadopoulos, H., Proedrou, K., Vovk, V. & Gammerman, A. (2002). Inductive confidence machines for
  regression. *ECML*, 345–356.
- Lei, J., G'Sell, M., Rinaldo, A., Tibshirani, R. J. & Wasserman, L. (2018). Distribution-free
  predictive inference for regression. *JASA* 113(523), 1094–1111.
- Romano, Y., Patterson, E. & Candès, E. J. (2019). Conformalized quantile regression. *NeurIPS*.
- Barber, R. F., Candès, E. J., Ramdas, A. & Tibshirani, R. J. (2021). Predictive inference with
  the jackknife+. *Annals of Statistics* 49(1), 486–507.
- Kim, B., Xu, C. & Barber, R. F. (2020). Predictive inference is free with the
  jackknife+-after-bootstrap. *NeurIPS*.
- Tibshirani, R. J., Barber, R. F., Candès, E. J. & Ramdas, A. (2019). Conformal prediction under
  covariate shift. *NeurIPS*.
- Barber, R. F., Candès, E. J., Ramdas, A. & Tibshirani, R. J. (2023). Conformal prediction beyond
  exchangeability. *Annals of Statistics* 51(2), 816–845.
- Gibbs, I. & Candès, E. J. (2021). Adaptive conformal inference under distribution shift. *NeurIPS*.
- Gibbs, I. & Candès, E. J. (2024). Conformal inference for online prediction with arbitrary
  distribution shifts. *JMLR* 25(162), 1–36.
- Bhatnagar, A., Wang, H., Xiong, C. & Bai, Y. (2023). Improved online conformal prediction via
  strongly adaptive online learning. *ICML*. Reference code: `salesforce/online_conformal`
  (Apache-2.0).
- Gauthier, E., Bach, F. & Jordan, M. I. (2026). Adaptive coverage policies in conformal
  prediction. *AISTATS*. arXiv:2510.04318.
- Angelopoulos, A. N. & Bates, S. (2023). Conformal prediction: a gentle introduction.
  *Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning* 16(4), 494–591.

## Extreme-value theory

- Pickands, J. (1975). Statistical inference using extreme order statistics. *Annals of Statistics*
  3(1), 119–131.
- Coles, S. (2001). *An Introduction to Statistical Modeling of Extreme Values*. Springer.

## Quantile regression

- Koenker, R. & Bassett, G. (1978). Regression quantiles. *Econometrica* 46(1), 33–50.
- Meinshausen, N. (2006). Quantile regression forests. *JMLR* 7, 983–999.
- Chernozhukov, V., Fernández-Val, I. & Galichon, A. (2010). Quantile and probability curves
  without crossing. *Econometrica* 78(3), 1093–1125.
- Arik, S. Ö. & Pfister, T. (2021). TabNet: attentive interpretable tabular learning. *AAAI*.

## Probabilistic regression

- Duan, T., Avati, A., Ding, D. Y., Thai, K. K., Basu, S., Ng, A. & Schuler, A. (2020). NGBoost:
  natural gradient boosting for probabilistic prediction. *ICML*.
- Sprangers, O., Schelter, S. & de Rijke, M. (2021). Probabilistic gradient boosting machines for
  large-scale probabilistic regression. *KDD*.
- Brophy, J. & Lowd, D. (2022). Instance-based uncertainty estimation for gradient-boosted
  regression trees. *NeurIPS*. Reference code: `jjbrophy47/ibug`.
- Han, X., Zheng, H. & Zhou, M. (2022). CARD: classification and regression diffusion models.
  *NeurIPS*.
- Choi et al. (2026). Uncertainty estimation via hyperspherical confidence mapping. *ICLR*.
  arXiv:2605.05964.
- Beltran-Velez, N., Grande, A. A., Nazaret, A., Kucukelbir, A. & Blei, D. (2024). Treeffuser:
  probabilistic predictions via conditional diffusions with gradient-boosted trees. *NeurIPS*.

## Evaluation

- Winkler, R. L. (1972). A decision-theoretic approach to interval estimation. *JASA* 67(337),
  187–191.
- Gneiting, T. & Raftery, A. E. (2007). Strictly proper scoring rules, prediction, and estimation.
  *JASA* 102(477), 359–378.
- Gneiting, T. & Ranjan, R. (2011). Comparing density forecasts using threshold- and
  quantile-weighted scoring rules. *JBES* 29(3), 411–422.
- Hersbach, H. (2000). Decomposition of the continuous ranked probability score for ensemble
  prediction systems. *Weather and Forecasting* 15(5), 559–570.
- Ferro, C. A. T. (2014). Fair scores for ensemble forecasts. *QJRMS* 140(683), 1917–1923.
- Khosravi, A., Nahavandi, S. & Creighton, D. (2011). Construction of optimal prediction intervals
  for load forecasting problems. *IEEE Trans. Power Systems* 26(3).
- Levi, D., Gispan, L., Giladi, N. & Fetaya, E. (2022). Evaluating and calibrating uncertainty
  prediction in regression tasks. *Sensors* 22(15), 5540.

## Tuning

- Akiba, T., Sano, S., Yanase, T., Ohta, T. & Koyama, M. (2019). Optuna: a next-generation
